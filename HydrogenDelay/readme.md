### H₂ Delay (Cost of Waiting) Study

This study investigates the **cost of delaying hydrogen (H₂-DRI) adoption** in India's steel sector and its impact on long-term decarbonization. The analysis quantifies how postponing the introduction of green hydrogen affects technology deployment, CCS requirements, and the levelized cost of steel production under different emissions targets and infrastructure expansion rates.

#### Study Design

The model evaluates combinations of:

* **H₂-DRI start years:** **2030, 2035, 2040, and 2045**
* **Emission caps:** **1.6, 1.8, and 2.0 tCO₂/t steel**
* **Three infrastructure build-rate scenarios:**

  * **Low:** 10 Mt steel/year capacity addition, **1.0 Mt H₂/year** peak electrolyser capacity
  * **Medium:** 15 Mt steel/year capacity addition, **1.5 Mt H₂/year** peak electrolyser capacity
  * **High:** 20 Mt steel/year capacity addition, **2.0 Mt H₂/year** peak electrolyser capacity

This results in **36 optimization scenarios**, while all other model assumptions remain unchanged (central technology learning parameters and **6% annual scrap growth**).

#### Model Outputs

For each scenario, the model reports:

* Levelized cost of steel production (LCOP)
* 2050 H₂-DRI capacity
* 2050 CCS deployment
* Total emissions
* Hydrogen-induced emission reduction in 2050

Together, these results quantify the **economic and technological cost of delaying hydrogen deployment**, illustrating how later H₂ adoption increases reliance on alternative decarbonization pathways and affects India's least-cost transition to low-carbon steel.
