**Reading conventions.** Feasibility figures use all 46,656 scenarios.
Cost figures ($/t crude steel) use only the calibrated subset — the
scenarios whose 2050 grid emission factor lies in 350–550 gCO2/kWh, where
the coupled electricity tariff is inside its calibration anchors. All
differences are *paired*: computed over scenarios feasible at both settings
of the lever being varied, with the pair count reported. A feasibility
share such as 0.685 is the fraction of the remaining lever combinations in
the design that solve — not a real-world probability. The design is a
complete factorial, so these shares are balanced and comparable to each
other.
